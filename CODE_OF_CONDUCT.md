# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `presidio-rs` respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, employer, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and directly;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- provide evidence for technical and security claims;
- accept correction without retaliation;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and disclosure boundaries;
- attribute prior work and third-party material;
- avoid flooding maintainers with duplicate, low-value, or unverified reports; and
- prioritize user safety over winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberate misrepresentation of test, benchmark, security, or provenance evidence;
- knowingly submitting copied, license-incompatible, or unattributed material;
- using automated systems to create spam, synthetic community activity, or unreviewed contributions;
- retaliating against good-faith security reports or review feedback; and
- disrupting project spaces after being asked to stop.

## Technical disagreement

Security and privacy work often involves disagreement about threat models, tradeoffs, and evidence. Strong disagreement is allowed. Contempt, intimidation, and fabricated certainty are not.

Maintainers may close discussions that become repetitive, abusive, promotional, or disconnected from actionable project work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers using the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, reviews, project meetings, and public communications made on behalf of the Project.

## Attribution

This document is informed by common open-source community standards, including Contributor Covenant principles, but is written specifically for this Project.
